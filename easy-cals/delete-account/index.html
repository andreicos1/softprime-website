How to Delete Your Easy Cals Account

Delete from the app

  1. Open Easy Cals and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down to Account Actions and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the message and tap Delete to confirm.

You'll be signed out right away and your account will be switched off.

If you can't open the app

Email andrei.easy-cals@softprimeapps.com with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address linked to your Apple ID sign-in, or include that address in the message. We'll process your request and email you when it's done.

What happens after you delete

  • Right away: your account is switched off and you can't sign in to it.
  • For 30 days: your account and data are kept in case you change your mind. Email support during this time if you want your account back.
  • After 30 days: your account and all its data are permanently deleted. This includes your profile, food logs, weight entries and linked devices. It can't be undone.

If you sign in with the same Apple ID after 30 days, you'll start with a new, empty account.

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Apple handles billing, so cancel it yourself:

iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Easy Cals → Cancel Subscription.
